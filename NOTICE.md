# Public showcase boundary

This repository intentionally contains only:

- product and engineering documentation;
- architecture and domain diagrams;
- selected, limited excerpts from the original implementation;
- selected test excerpts demonstrating domain behavior.

It intentionally excludes:

- the complete application source and templates;
- databases, backups, customer/product records, and uploaded media;
- secrets, credentials, local paths, deployment values, and environment files;
- complete migrations, static assets, and production infrastructure;
- any material not required for professional evaluation.

The excerpts retain their original domain behavior but are not a runnable distribution of the product.
